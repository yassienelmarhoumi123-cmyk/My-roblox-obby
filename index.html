👁️ Verbs of Perception + Object + Present Participle

Übungen zu Buchseite 19 (Aufgabe 8 & 9).

📌 Grammatik-Formel:
Verb der Wahrnehmung (see, hear, notice, watch, smell, spot, feel) + Objekt + Verb mit -ing.
Beispiel: Tom spotted a koala mother feeding her baby. / I saw a ferry arriving.

🧩 Participle Clauses (Verkürzung von Relativsätzen)

Übungen zu Buchseite 18 & 172.

📌 Aktiv vs. Passiv:
• Aktiv (Present Participle = -ing): The man who was driving the car → The man driving the car.
• Passiv (Past Participle = -ed / 3. Form): An event which is watched by millions → An event watched by millions.
